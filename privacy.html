Privacy policy — TimeFlew

Developer: Tridip Choudhury
Contact: tridipchoudhury88@gmail.com
Effective: 17 November 2026

What stays on your device

The app saves your approximate time in the feed, card-transition totals, and feed position in local app storage. Time is estimated only while the feed is visible and you have interacted recently. The app does not measure other apps or your attention.

What we collect

This version has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, cloud database, or runtime AI. The app does not send your feed history or usage totals to us. Text and simple geometric content are included in the app and do not require an internet connection.

Fictional statistics

Country scrolling averages are fictional entertainment values, not measurements of users or residents. Population figures are estimates included in the app. Neither is calculated from your personal history.

Storage and deletion

Reset personal history clears the recorded feed-time and transition totals on this installation. Feed position is stored separately. Removing the app may remove its local data. Device backups and restores may retain or restore app data according to your operating-system settings. The app provides no account or cross-device sync service.

Optional links and support

Opening a support or privacy website uses your browser and that site may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address. If you contact support, you choose which information to send. Do not include sensitive information in a support message.

Changes

This explanation describes the current text-and-geometry version. New features such as external videos, accounts, advertising or analytics would require a fresh privacy review and an updated explanation before release.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. The website includes no advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its hosting practices.
