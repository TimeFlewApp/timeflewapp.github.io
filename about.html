A small explanation

Absolutely nothing to prove.

A feed about nothing, with an honest little reminder that you are scrolling. No score to improve. No streak to protect. You can leave whenever you like.

What is in the app?

1,024 original short posts, horizontal companion sentences, hidden tap responses and simple geometric artwork. The text was created with AI assistance and edited for the app. No third-party images, videos or audio are bundled.

What is real?

Your approximate feed time and transition totals are stored on your device. Country scrolling averages are fictional entertainment values; population figures are estimates. The app does not measure other apps or attention.
