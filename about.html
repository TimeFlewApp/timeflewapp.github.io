A small explanation

Absolutely nothing to prove.

TimeFlew is an offline humour app: a feed about nothing, with an honest little reminder that you are scrolling. No score to improve. No streak to protect. You can leave whenever you like.

What is in the app?

Thousands of original short posts, horizontal companion sentences, hidden tap responses and simple geometric artwork. No third-party images, videos or audio are bundled.

What is real?

Your approximate feed time and transition totals are stored on your device. Country scrolling averages are fictional entertainment values; population figures are estimates. The app does not measure other apps or attention.
