Report a problem

Include the app version, phone model, operating-system version and what happened. A short screen recording can help with movement issues. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Does the app need internet?

The bundled text feed works offline. Optional support and website links require internet access.

Why do posts repeat?

The app contains a finite collection. It reshuffles after each cycle.

How do I reset my statistics?

Open About in the app and choose Reset personal history. This clears recorded feed-time and transition totals on this installation. Feed position is stored separately.

Accounts and deletion

There is no account to delete and no account data stored on our servers. Device backups and app removal are controlled by your operating system. See Privacy for details.
